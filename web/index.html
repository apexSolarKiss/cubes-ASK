82–94%

Red and orange stay nearly opaque, so they read as red and orange. This sets the lowest opacity they may take, from 70% up; their highest is 94%. Every other color takes 38 to 87%.

1 ×

1 × is a 1 px line on a picture 1080 px high. Changing it re-draws every cube.

settings

A picture holds up to 30,000 cubes. Then it is full and stays as it is: save it, or clear to start another. On a phone in full window, clear then takes the place of play, and for the 8 seconds a clear can be undone, undo shows beside save.

On the canvas, space pauses or plays. Esc leaves full window.
